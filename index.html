Welcome to Our Clinic

Our Clinic Appointment System allows patients to book and manage their medical appointments online.

Project Introduction

The Clinic Appointment System is a web-based application designed to simplify the process of scheduling and managing medical appointments.

The system will allow patients to:

  • Register
  • View available doctors and appointment times
  • Book appointments
  • Manage their bookings online

Clinic staff and doctors will also be able to:

  • Manage schedules
  • View upcoming appointments

Project Goals

The system aims to:

  • Reduce long waiting times
  • Minimize appointment conflicts
  • Improve the organization of clinic services
  • Improve the overall efficiency of clinic services

Important Information

Patients should arrive at the clinic on time for their scheduled appointments.

Patients who cannot attend their appointments should reschedule or cancel them in advance.
